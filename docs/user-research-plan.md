# Chapter 8 — Low-Cost Solo Founder User Research Plan

## Overview & Execution Strategy
This research plan is designed for a solo founder operating under a zero to very low budget (₹0 to ₹5,000 total allocation). It avoids expensive research agencies or paid user panels, leveraging direct Advocate interviews, manual concierge testing, synthetic document walk-throughs, and free open-source prototyping tools.

---

## 1. Candidate Screening Criteria & Target Recruitment

### Target Profile
- **Primary Persona**: Independent Property Advocates, Title Search Specialists, Bank Panel Advocates in Tier-1/Tier-2 Indian cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune).
- **Secondary Profile**: Junior/Senior Litigation Associates at mid-sized law firms handling property dispute bundles.

### Screening Checklist
- [x] Must perform at least 3 property title searches or court matter bundle reviews per week.
- [x] Must regularly handle multi-document bundles (Sale Deeds, Pahani/RTC, EC, Affidavits).
- [x] Must currently use MS Word / PDF viewer for report drafting.
- [x] Expresses frustration with manual reading time or risk of extent/name discrepancies.

---

## 2. Interview Protocol & Questions

### Structure (30-Minute Semi-Structured Interview)

#### A. Background & Current Process (10 Mins)
1. "Walk me through how you execute a property title search when a client or bank hands you a bundle of 15 documents."
2. "What specific documents do you look at first, and why?"
3. "How do you track the flow of title and verify that no link deed is missing?"
4. "What tools do you currently use to read, highlight, and take notes on these deeds?"

#### B. Pain Points & Risk Exposure (10 Mins)
5. "What is the most tedious or time-consuming part of this workflow?"
6. "Have you ever encountered a situation where a subtle discrepancy in land extent or boundary went unnoticed until late in the transaction? How did that happen?"
7. "How many hours do you spend on average drafting a single Title Search Report from scratch?"

#### C. Solution Feedback & WTP Probe (10 Mins)
8. "If a desktop workspace could automatically extract parties, dates, survey numbers, and build a verified title flow timeline with split-screen page citations, how would that change your daily process?"
9. "What would prevent you from trusting such a tool?"
10. "If this tool saved you 4+ hours per title report and cost ₹200 per matter, would that fit into your practice budget?"

---

## 3. Workflow Observation Checklist & Task Protocol

### Practical Observation Session (Concierge / Synthetic Prototype Test)
Provide the Advocate with a synthetic 5-document property bundle (Sale Deed 1985, Mother Deed 1970, Partition Deed 2002, RTC Extract 2018, Encumbrance Certificate).

### Observer Checklist
- [ ] Time taken to locate Survey Number across all deeds.
- [ ] Time taken to identify the missing link deed / extent discrepancy in the synthetic bundle.
- [ ] Points of hesitation or confusion when reviewing Indic scanned pages.
- [ ] Verification method used (does the Advocate cross-check quotes against original PDF?).
- [ ] Spontaneous reactions to split-screen bounding-box citation highlights.

---

## 4. Feedback Form & Quantitative Scoring Method

### Post-Test Feedback Form (1–5 Likert Scale)

1. **Clarity of Title Flow Timeline**: (1 = Confusing, 5 = Crystal Clear)
2. **Confidence in Split-Screen Citation Accuracy**: (1 = Low Trust, 5 = Complete Trust)
3. **Speed Improvement over Manual Process**: (1 = No Faster, 5 = 5x Faster)
4. **Likelihood of Daily Use in Practice**: (1 = Unlikely, 5 = Extremely Likely)
5. **Per-Matter Pricing Perception (₹200/matter)**: (1 = Too Expensive, 5 = Excellent Value)

### Target Passing Threshold for Product-Market Signal
- Average score across daily Advocates must be **>= 4.2 out of 5.0**.
- At least 70% of interviewed Advocates must agree to test a free pilot on their next live matter.
