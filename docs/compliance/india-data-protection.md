# India Data Protection Compliance (DPDP Alignment)

## Privacy & Personal Data Minimization
1. **Purpose Limitation**: Personal data extracted from public property records (names, addresses) is processed solely for advocate title diligence workflows.
2. **Access Control**: Scoped to authenticated matter members. Cross-tenant leakage is strictly blocked.
3. **Data Minimization**: Aadhaar, PAN, and banking credentials found in raw documents are masked prior to indexing.
