# Property Intelligence & Chain of Title Workflows

## 1. Property Identifier Structure
- **Survey Number**: e.g., `Survey No. 42/1 Hissa 2`
- **Land Extent**: e.g., `2 Acres 24 Guntas (104,544 Sq.Ft)`
- **Jurisdiction**: State (Karnataka), District (Bengaluru Rural), Taluk (Devanahalli), Village (Singahalli).

## 2. Property Timeline Chain of Title
- **Chronology Engine**: Orders historical deeds by execution/registration date over a 13-to-30 year period.
- **Title Chain Events**:
  - `SALE_DEED` (Transfer of absolute title)
  - `MORTGAGE_DEED` (Simple/Equitable Mortgage encumbrance)
  - `RELEASE_DEED` (Reconveyance/Discharge of mortgage)
  - `GIFT_DEED` / `INHERITANCE` (Family settlement or succession)
