# Evidence-Backed Property Title Search Report

## Title Search Report Structure
1. **Executive Summary & Title Opinion**: Brief summary of 30-year chain of title and Advocate opinion status.
2. **Property Details & Boundaries**: Survey #, Hissa #, Extent, Boundaries (North, South, East, West).
3. **Documents Reviewed Inventory**: Chronological table of verified deeds (Sale Deed, EC, Khata, RTC).
4. **Flow of Title & Chronological Timeline**: Year-by-year transaction chain (1985 to present).
5. **Encumbrances & Contradictions**: Active mortgages, court litigation flags, extent discrepancies.
6. **Missing Information & Title Gaps**: Missing deeds or unverified EC years.
7. **Disclaimers & Reviewer Signature**: Standard legal disclaimers stating report relies on provided documents and requires human advocate sign-off.
