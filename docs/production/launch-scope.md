# Production Launch Scope & Capability Boundary

## Supported Capabilities for Release v0.1.0

### Supported Jurisdictions & Regions
- **Karnataka**: Bangalore Urban, Bangalore Rural, Devanahalli (Kaveri 2.0 land records).
- **Maharashtra**: Pune, Mumbai Suburban (MahaBhulekh 7/12 mutation extracts).
- **National**: eCourts litigation metadata search for High Courts & District Courts.

### Supported Languages & Document Formats
- **Languages**: English, Devanagari (Hindi, Marathi).
- **Document Types**: PDF (Text & Scanned), Image Deeds (PNG/JPEG), RTC Extracts, EC Certificates.

### Explicitly Unsupported / Out-of-Scope Capabilities
- Auto-execution of real property transfers or electronic court filings (Human-in-the-Loop mandatory).
- Unsupported regional languages (e.g., Tamil, Telugu) — routed to manual advocate review.
