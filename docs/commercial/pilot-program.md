# Enterprise Customer Pilot Program & Success Criteria

## 14-Day Pilot Success Gate Criteria
1. **Title Due Diligence Velocity**: 100% of pilot property title deeds processed in < 30 minutes.
2. **Citation Traceability**: 100% of generated title report findings contain verifiable page locators (`EVID-AI-03`).
3. **Accuracy & Quality**: Zero critical hallucinated encumbrances or false party matches (`EVL-001`).
4. **User Adoption**: Advocates complete at least 5 full title search reports independently during pilot window.
