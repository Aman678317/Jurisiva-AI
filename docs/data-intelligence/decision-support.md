# Explainable Decision Support Framework

## Decision Support Principles
- **No Autonomous Legal Decisions**: The system provides structured risk indicators and evidence summaries; final due diligence decisions are made strictly by the advocate.
- **Explainability**: Every risk flag includes source documents, date ranges, competing claims, and confidence scores.
- **Missing Data Visibility**: Clearly highlights gaps in title chain (e.g., "Missing 7/12 extract for period 1995-2002").
