# AI Risk Classification & Model Approval Controls

## AI Risk Tier Classification

| Risk Tier | Feature Examples | Human Approval Required | Approval Authority |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **`LOW`** | Search query expansion, document summarization | No | AI Eng Lead |
| **`MEDIUM`** | Precedent judgment retrieval, court event extraction | No (Audit Log) | Lead Advocate |
| **`HIGH`** | Encumbrance certificate verification, property title extraction | Mandatory | Lead Advocate + CTO |
| **`CRITICAL`** | Autonomous legal advice, automatic deed modification | FORBIDDEN | Board Approval |

Autonomous high-risk legal decision making is strictly prohibited across the platform.
