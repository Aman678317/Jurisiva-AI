# Privacy Data Map & Retention Policy

## Data Minimization & Retention Schedule
- **Original User Documents**: Retained while matter is active + 7 years (configurable per organization).
- **Extracted Page Text & OCR**: Cascading deletion upon document removal.
- **AI Run Logs & Cost Telemetry**: Retained for 1 year for billing and performance auditing.
- **Audit Logs**: Retained for 5 years for legal compliance and security investigation.
