# Privacy Governance & DPDP Act Alignment

## Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act Compliance Principles
1. **Notice & Consent**: Transparent processing notices presented during advocate organization onboarding.
2. **Purpose Limitation**: Personal data contained within property title deeds is processed strictly for legal due diligence workflows.
3. **Zero Training Exposure**: Customer PDF title deeds and advocate research queries are NEVER used for AI model training.
4. **Data Principal Rights**: Data export and deletion propagation executed within 30 days of verified customer request.
