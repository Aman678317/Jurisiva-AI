# Event Model & Property Timeline Tracking

## Event Types & Chain of Title Chronology
- **`SALE_DEED_EXECUTION`**: Transfer of title between seller (executant) and buyer (claimant).
- **`MORTGAGE_CREATION`**: Encumbrance registered with financial institution.
- **`MORTGAGE_RELEASE`**: Encumbrance discharge deed registered.
- **`MUTATION_ENTRY`**: Revenue record entry in 7/12 or RTC Form 16.
- **`COURT_LITIGATION`**: Civil suit filed in local district court.
