# Source Authority Model

## Authority Hierarchy Levels

- **LEVEL 1 — PRIMARY OFFICIAL**: Gazette notifications, Supreme Court & High Court judgments, registered Land Registry deeds.
- **LEVEL 2 — AUTHORIZED GOVERNMENT / REGULATORY**: RERA portal listings, MCA company records, municipal tax assessments.
- **LEVEL 3 — REPUTABLE SECONDARY**: Published legal commentaries, law reports, official news notifications.
- **LEVEL 4 — USER-PROVIDED**: Uploaded title deeds, client sale agreements, private encumbrance certificates.
- **LEVEL 5 — DISCOVERY ONLY**: Public web search results, unverified external listings.

---

## Authority vs Truth Principle
An official source record (e.g. eCourts order) is authoritative for procedural events, but may be incomplete or outdated regarding current property ownership. The system displays source authority levels explicitly on all UI search results.
