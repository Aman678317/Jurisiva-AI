# Customer Interview & Feedback Framework

## Structured Interview Questions for Advocate Cohorts
1. **Workflow Efficiency**: How much time did the platform save during 30-year title deed verification compared to manual inspection?
2. **Citation Trust**: Did every copilot answer clearly point to the exact page and paragraph in the uploaded deed?
3. **Contradiction Detection**: How effective was the extent mismatch and unreleased mortgage conflict detector?
4. **Feature Gaps**: What additional government source portals or state registries would most increase your productivity?
