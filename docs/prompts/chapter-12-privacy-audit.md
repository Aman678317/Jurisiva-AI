# Chapter 12 Prompt — Privacy Audit

```markdown
Act as a Privacy Engineering Lead.

Map every personal-data flow.

For each:
- collection
- purpose
- storage
- processing
- AI sharing
- vendor sharing
- retention
- deletion
- access

Identify:
- unnecessary collection
- uncontrolled retention
- unsafe logging
- unapproved third-party processing

Separate verified legal requirements from assumptions.
Flag counsel-review items.
```
