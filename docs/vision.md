# Product Vision & Founder Blueprint

## Vision Statement
To build the premier India-first AI-powered Legal & Property Intelligence Platform that empowers legal professionals, Advocates, title examiners, and in-house counsel to analyze complex documents, execute rigorous due diligence, reconcile property records, and generate auditable, evidence-backed legal work products with high precision.

## Strategic Position & Inspiration Benchmark
- **Product Philosophy Benchmark**: Inspired by the professional workflow standards of leading global platforms like Harvey AI (matter-centric workflows, citation-grounded RAG, structured document intelligence, enterprise security).
- **Originality & Autonomy**: The platform is built entirely from scratch with original code, explicit India-first domain models, and zero reliance on proprietary third-party trade secrets or code.
- **India-First Focus**: Tailored specifically to Indian legal systems, regional land record formats, multilingual scanned documents, and Indian evidence laws (Indian Evidence Act / Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam).

## Core Target Personas
1. **Indian Law-Firm Associate**: Needs fast document synthesis, clause review, contradiction detection, and citation-aware research across massive case bundles.
2. **Property Lawyer & Due-Diligence Specialist**: Needs title chain reconciliation, land record verification (Pahani/RTC, Khasra, Encumbrance Certificates), boundary/survey analysis, and automated title search report drafting.
3. **In-House Legal Professional**: Needs contract risk scoring, compliance tracking, lease abstraction, and vendor agreement auditing.

## Guiding Principles
- **Evidence-First**: No ungrounded claims. Every finding links directly to source page and snippet.
- **Human Authority**: AI assists, human decides. Clear UI distinction between Source Fact, AI Extraction, AI Inference, and Human Verification.
- **Privacy & Security**: Matter-isolated multi-tenant storage, complete audit log trail, enterprise-grade RBAC.
