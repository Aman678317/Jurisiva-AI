# Legal & Property Source Registry & Authority Hierarchy

## Source Authority Classes

| Authority Tier | Source Description | Verification Status | Example Sources |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Primary Official** | Government land registries & Supreme/High Court orders | `VERIFIED` | Kaveri 2.0 RTC, MahaBhulekh 7/12, eCourts Judgments |
| **Tier 2: Official Gazette** | State notifications & statutory amendment gazettes | `VERIFIED` | State Revenue Department Circulars |
| **Tier 3: Secondary Commentary** | Advocate treatises & legal analytical commentaries | `PENDING_VERIFICATION` | Approved Legal Treatises & Legal Journals |
| **Tier 4: Derived AI Summary** | AI-generated research syntheses | `UNVERIFIED` | Copilot Summary (Requires Advocate Review) |

AI-generated summaries can NEVER be classified as Primary Official sources.
