# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Target Audience
- Primary: Property Advocates, Real-Estate Due-Diligence Lawyers, Title Examiners in India.
- Secondary: Law Firm Associates, In-House Counsel.

## Core Features & User Stories
1. **Matter Management**: Create, view, search, and archive matters containing client files.
2. **Document Ingestion & OCR**: Process PDF, scanned images, TIFFs; extract clean text layer with Indic language support.
3. **Evidence Extraction**: Extract Survey Numbers, Extent, Parties, Boundaries, Encumbrance details, Consideration values.
4. **Timeline & Contradiction Detection**: Build chronological title history, highlight missing link deeds and extent mismatches.
5. **Citation-Aware RAG Chat**: Ask questions against matter files with exact page citations and visual bounding highlights.
6. **Human Review & Export**: Verify extractions, mark status, and export clean Title Search Reports (PDF/DOCX).

## Success Criteria
- 100% citation traceable outputs.
- Reduction in title review time from 8 hours to < 2.5 hours.
