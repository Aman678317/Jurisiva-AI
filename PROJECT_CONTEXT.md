# Project Context: India-First Legal & Property Intelligence Platform

## Project Overview
This project is an original, India-first, AI-powered Legal & Property Intelligence Platform designed to assist legal professionals, property due-diligence teams, advocates, and in-house counsel in analyzing complex documents, conducting legal and title research, managing matters, detecting contradictions across document bundles, and generating evidence-grounded work products.

## Core Architectural & Product Philosophy
1. **Human-in-the-Loop**: All AI outputs serve as drafting and research support for qualified human professionals. The human expert retains decision authority for all consequential legal and property matters.
2. **Evidence First & Strict Citation**: Every material claim, finding, or summary generated by the AI must map directly to traceable source text (document, page, paragraph/line offset).
3. **Original-Source Preservation**: Uploaded files and original legal/property records are immutable source truths. Extracted text and derived analytics operate as indexed overlays.
4. **Free/Open-Source First**: Core capabilities rely on open source tools, frameworks, and self-hostable models wherever feasible, maintaining low operating overhead without premature cloud vendor lock-in.
5. **Small Increments & Continuous Testing**: Development proceeds chapter-by-chapter following strict phase gates, verified by unit/integration/UX test suites.
6. **No Invented APIs or Scraped Government Gates**: Ground truth integrations rely strictly on user-provided documents, uploaded public records, or official APIs. No undocumented or brittle web-scraping.
7. **Model Interchangeability**: Model orchestration layers use standardized abstractions (LiteLLM/Ollama/OpenAI-compatible interfaces) ensuring fast model swapping without rewriting business logic.
8. **Zero Trust for Content**: All processed documents, OCR text, and retrieved vector chunks are treated as untrusted data subject to strict sanitization, prompt injection defense, and isolated parsing.

## Target User Segments (Initial Focus)
- **Indian Law-Firm Associates**: Managing document discovery, contract review, and case law research.
- **Property Lawyers & Due Diligence Specialists**: Analyzing property title chains, sale deeds, encumbrance certificates, mutation extracts (Pahani/RTC), and RTC records.
- **In-House Legal & Compliance Professionals**: Reviewing commercial agreements, regulatory filings, and corporate governance documents.
